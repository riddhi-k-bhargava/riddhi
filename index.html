01

Walmart

Onboarding Growth Experimentation 0→1→N

7,000 sellers to 45,000. One redesign.

From a 6-week gated application to a 10-minute self-serve experience

Growth and quality were treated as competing goals. Leadership wanted to expand the seller base but kept onboarding gated. The assumption was that gates protected program quality. Removing them felt like a risk.

Ran controlled experiments to challenge that assumption. Parallelized onboarding tasks to observe how sellers actually moved through them without any imposed order. Used that behavioral data to design a sequenced, self-serve experience. Partnered with marketing to embed program enrollment at the moment a seller joins the platform, making WFS fulfillment the natural default choice. WFS gives third-party sellers access to faster shipping speeds, making Walmart.com more competitive for consumers who expect fast delivery. The result strengthened WFS's market position and changed the internal conversation from "how do we protect quality" to "how do we scale faster."

~53% GMV CAGR over 5 years
7× seller base growth
Stronger WFS market position

Walmart

Monetization Incentives AI/ML 1→N

From scattered tools and email approvals to an incentive platform built for scale

$x00M in annual promotional spend was managed across disparate systems: no unified view, no ROI visibility, and an approval process fragmented across email threads.

Started with a POC scoped to unblock adoption and test assumptions while business strategy was still taking shape. Once the strategy clarified, rebuilt the approach: tied incentives to specific seller actions to nudge performance and program adoption, then designed a self-serve platform where account managers set goals, get AI-suggested audience and incentive mix, and route for finance approval in one workflow. ROI became a first-class metric. Every dollar of spend now has a projected and actual return attached.

$x00M annual spend managed
48% ROI increase

03

Walmart

Catalog AI/ML Platform infrastructure Compliance

More products on the shelf. Better discovery for shoppers.

From unclear categorization and days-long compliance bottlenecks to listing in minutes and unlocking product categories that made Walmart Marketplace more competitive

Flat categories made it hard to collect the right information at listing time and blocked entire product types from the marketplace. The result was friction for sellers, compliance gaps, and categories consumers simply could not find on Walmart.com.

Rebuilt the catalog around granular product-type specs so the right fields appeared for the right product. Built search-and-match into the listing workflow so sellers could reuse prior compliance decisions instantly rather than waiting days for a fresh review. Added ML pre-classification with a live feedback loop to catch what still needed human review. Together, these changes unlocked regulated goods, resale, and big-and-bulky categories that had been effectively invisible on the marketplace. Broader assortment means consumers find more of what they are looking for on Walmart.com. New product categories are a consumer experience outcome, not just a seller metric.

60% faster product listing
New categories unlocked for consumers

04

SAP Ariba

B2B SaaS 0→1 Procurement Risk

From a one-time risk assessment to risk intelligence embedded in every sourcing decision

Enterprise customers ran supplier risk assessments once a year in a tool that sat completely outside their sourcing workflow. Risk was a siloed audit, disconnected from the daily decisions where it would have actually mattered.

Identified a post-launch adoption stall as a product-market fit problem, not a customer success failure. The product was solving for a moment (the annual review) instead of a workflow (ongoing sourcing). Pivoted to continuous monitoring with configurable risk scorecards, then embedded risk scores directly into source-to-pay: filtering supplier search, informing contract terms, triggering automatic reassessment when signals changed. Risk stopped being something teams visited and became something that traveled with every sourcing decision.

150% revenue growth
2× customer base
Gartner MQ + Forrester Wave recognized leader
