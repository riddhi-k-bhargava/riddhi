Onboarding Growth Experimentation 0→1→N

From niche to mainstream. One redesign.

From a 6-week gated application to a 10-minute self-serve experience

7× growthSeller base, 5 years
Double-digit CAGRGMV sustained 5 years
2 in 3 top sellersNow choose Program first
Before · locked steps, weeks apart
V2 · steps open at once
V3 · join once, sell everywhere
ChallengeLeadership believed a gated Program was a quality signal. Friction was considered a feature, not a problem.
ApproachRan experiments to test whether removing the gate would flood the Program — it didn't. Parallelized tasks to observe natural user sequencing, redesigned the experience around what users actually did, and embedded Program enrollment at Marketplace join to make it the default, not a second decision.
