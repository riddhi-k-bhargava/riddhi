How it decides

Timing is anchored to the CDC "Learn the Signs. Act Early." milestone checklists (2022 revision, the 75th percentile ages at which most babies show a skill) and the WHO windows for the six gross motor milestones. It does not blindly trust the "using" switch: if your baby has likely aged past an item's useful window, it moves to pack away with the typical age noted. For safety-adjacent items like car seat inserts and swaddles, it points you to the product's own weight and height limits and current safe-sleep guidance rather than a hard age. The on-track line is a non-diagnostic reflection of what you entered against those public references. It is not a screen and cannot see your child.

Not medical advice. This is a working prototype for organizing baby gear, not a medical or developmental tool. Milestone references are public population averages and every baby is different. Practice ideas like propping to sit are for supervised, awake time only, never for sleep and never unattended. For questions about your child's health, development, feeding, or safe sleep, contact a qualified clinician. Independent personal project by Riddhi Bhargava, not affiliated with the CDC, the WHO, or any product brand.
