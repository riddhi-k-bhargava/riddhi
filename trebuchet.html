How to play

Trebuchet

  • Each question has a number for an answer. Wager a boulder (I, II or III), then fire your guess.
  • Land near the truth and the wall takes damage — dead-on triples your boulder, a telling blow doubles it, a glancing hit scores as-is. Bigger boulders hit harder.
  • Reach 25 damage across your 6 boulders to bring the wall down. You may lay 5 sieges in all.
